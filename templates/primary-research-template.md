## 07 — Evidence Classification

Use these labels consistently.

### Research-backed Finding
Direct participant evidence.

### Consolidated Insight
Synthesis across multiple observations or clusters.

### Positive Evidence / Preserve
Existing behavior or experience worth retaining.

### Participant Solution Suggestion
Participant-proposed solution; not automatically a requirement.

### Stakeholder / Participant Strategic Opinion
Strategic belief or opinion rather than behavioral evidence.

### Emerging Hypothesis
Promising pattern requiring validation.

### Research-informed Solution Hypothesis
Research validates the problem/need, but not necessarily the solution.

### Prototype-limited Finding
Potentially influenced by prototype limitations; do not generalize to production.

### Not Validated
Insufficient evidence for the current design or assumption.

### Partially Supported / Decision Required
Research supports part of the issue, but a Product / Business / Service decision remains open.

## 17 — Traceability Checklist

- [ ] Original research evidence can be identified.
- [ ] Interpretation is supported by that evidence.
- [ ] Actionable Insight describes a need/capability rather than a feature.
- [ ] Recommendation is distinguishable from Solution.

- [ ] Hypotheses are separated from confirmed findings.

# Golden Rules

1. Research is evidence—not every idea mentioned during research.
2. Participant suggestions are not automatically requirements.
3. A validated problem does not automatically validate a proposed solution.
4. Prototype limitations constrain what can be concluded.
5. Positive evidence matters; do not redesign what works without reason.
6. Confidence represents evidence strength, not business importance.
7. Keep Research, Insight, Recommendation, and Solution distinguishable.
8. Do not enlarge documentation simply because more ideas are possible.
9. When evidence is insufficient, classify the gap instead of filling it with assumptions.
10. Maintain traceability: Research → Insight → Recommendation → Solution.
